About Mr. Ziaulhye

Psychology Educator & Author

Mr. Ziaulhye Mirza Mahammed is a dedicated psychology educator, vlogger, and author with a passion for making psychological concepts accessible to everyone. With years of experience in mental health education, he has created a platform that combines scientific knowledge with practical applications for daily life.

His mission is to destigmatize mental health issues and provide evidence-based resources for personal growth and psychological well-being. Through his YouTube channel and books, he reaches thousands of viewers seeking to understand themselves and improve their mental health.

M.A. Psychology

Mr. Ziaulhye started his YouTube channel to bridge the gap between academic psychology and practical mental health advice. His books expand on these concepts, offering readers comprehensive guides to understanding and improving their psychological well-being.
